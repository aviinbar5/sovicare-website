Understand it first

The right approach starts with the cause.

Hair loss can have different causes, including hereditary patterns, illness, hormonal changes, medications, and stress. That’s why the same treatment won’t suit everyone.

Your provider will consider how your hair has changed, when it started, and your medical history. Some concerns may require testing or an in-person examination.

Treatment goals

Know what you’re working toward.

Depending on the cause of hair loss and the treatment selected, your plan may aim to:

Slow further hair loss. Help preserve existing hair where treatment is appropriate.

Support regrowth. Encourage growth in areas that may respond to treatment.

Improve fullness over time. Work toward better density through consistent treatment.

Not every treatment delivers every benefit. Your provider will explain what is realistic for your situation.

Give it time

Hair treatment is a routine.
Progress takes patience.

Hair grows gradually, so visible changes take months rather than days. Your response will depend on the cause of your hair loss, the treatment, and how consistently you use it.

Follow your treatment instructions and speak with your provider before changing your dose or stopping. Photos taken in consistent lighting can help you track changes over time.
