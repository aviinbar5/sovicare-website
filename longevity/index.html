Understand it first

Good decisions start with realistic expectations.

Longevity research is evolving. A treatment’s role in a biological process does not, by itself, prove that taking it will improve how you feel, reverse aging, or help you live longer.

Your provider can explain the evidence and uncertainties for the specific product being considered.

The foundations still matter.

Regular physical activity, adequate sleep, balanced nutrition, and routine medical care remain important parts of healthy aging. Discuss these alongside any treatment you are considering.
