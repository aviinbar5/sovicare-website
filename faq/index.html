Frequently asked questions.

How SoviCare works, what a provider decides, pricing and medication.

Categories

SoviCare is an online health platform that helps adults explore care for concerns including sexual health, hair loss, and wellness. You can share your health information from home and have it reviewed by a licensed healthcare provider, who determines whether a treatment is appropriate for you.

Start by choosing an area of care and completing an online health assessment. A licensed provider reviews your information and may ask follow-up questions before recommending a plan. If medication is appropriate, the provider can issue a prescription for fulfillment by a pharmacy.

Yes. SoviCare is being built for both men and women. Available treatments vary by health concern, medical suitability, and location.

A licensed healthcare provider is involved in every prescription decision. Depending on your needs and applicable requirements, the provider may ask for additional information or arrange a consultation before deciding on treatment.

No. Completing an assessment or paying for a consultation does not guarantee a prescription. Your provider will recommend treatment only if they determine it is medically appropriate.

Our planned areas of care include sexual health, hair loss, and wellness. Each treatment page will explain the options available for that concern. Your provider will consider your health history before recommending any prescription treatment.

The planned initial consultation fee is $25. Any additional treatment costs and applicable charges should be shown before you choose to proceed.

If a provider prescribes medication, the prescription will be sent to a pharmacy for fulfillment. Delivery options and timing depend on the treatment, pharmacy, and your location. You’ll receive the relevant details as part of your care process.

A compounded medication is prepared for a patient by a licensed pharmacist or other qualified compounder when a provider determines that a commercially available medication does not meet that patient’s medical needs. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved; the FDA does not review them for safety, effectiveness, or quality before they are marketed. Your provider can discuss whether a compounded option is appropriate for you.

Contact your healthcare provider with questions about your prescription, instructions, side effects, or changes in your health. For questions about your order or account, contact SoviCare support.
