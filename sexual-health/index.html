Understand it first

ED can have more than one cause.

Erectile dysfunction means difficulty getting or keeping an erection firm enough for sex. It can be connected to physical health, medications, stress, or a combination of factors.

Sharing your symptoms and medical history helps a provider assess your options. Sometimes, further testing or an in-person evaluation may be needed.

How treatment helps

Support for your body’s natural response.

Tadalafil and sildenafil help increase blood flow to the penis during sexual stimulation. They can make it easier to get and maintain an erection, but they do not cause an automatic erection or increase sexual desire.

The appropriate medication depends on your health, other medications, and treatment needs.

Important safety information

Tadalafil and sildenafil are not suitable for everyone. Common side effects include headache, flushing, and indigestion. Tadalafil may also cause back or muscle pain; sildenafil may cause vision changes.

Seek urgent medical care

Seek urgent medical care for an erection lasting more than four hours, chest pain during sexual activity, or sudden vision or hearing loss. Tell emergency medical staff which medication you took and when.
