Understanding tadalafil

How tadalafil helps.

Tadalafil is a prescription medication that treats erectile dysfunction by increasing blood flow to the penis during sexual stimulation.

It belongs to a group of medications called PDE5 inhibitors and contains the same active ingredient as Cialis®.

Good to know

Sexual stimulation is required. Tadalafil does not increase sexual desire or cause a continuous erection.

What to expect

Plan ahead, then enjoy more flexibility.

As-needed tadalafil is taken before anticipated sexual activity. Your provider will explain when to take it and how often you can use it.

Its effect can extend for up to 36 hours, although individual responses vary. This does not mean an erection lasts that long.

Take only as prescribed. Do not take extra doses because the first dose has not worked as expected.

Important safety information

Do not take tadalafil with nitrate medications, recreational nitrites known as “poppers,” or riociguat. These combinations can cause a dangerous drop in blood pressure.

Tell your provider about all medications and medical conditions, particularly heart disease, blood pressure problems, and kidney or liver disease. Excessive alcohol can increase dizziness and low blood pressure.

Seek immediate medical attention for an erection lasting more than four hours. Stop taking tadalafil and seek urgent care for sudden vision or hearing loss.

If you experience chest pain during sex, stop sexual activity and seek immediate medical help. Do not self-administer nitrates. Tell emergency clinicians when you last took tadalafil, as the interaction can persist beyond its treatment window.

Tadalafil does not prevent pregnancy or sexually transmitted infections. Read the full medication information before use.
